About Recipe Finder

Mission: help people cook what they already have. Recipe blogs bury the recipe under a life story, and most recipes call for something you don’t have. Recipe Finder starts from your kitchen, so you spend less money and throw out less food.

How to use Recipe Finder

1

⌨️ Search

Pick Ingredient, Cuisine or Dish name, type your search and press Enter. For several ingredients, separate them with commas.

2

🔻 Filter

Select the funnel to narrow by category, cuisine or dietary need. Dietary tags are estimated, so check labels if you have an allergy.

3

📖 Recipe popup

Select a card to see its instructions and ingredients. Select the × in the top-right corner to close it.

4

⭐ Saved recipes

Choose Save in the popup. Your saved list is in the sidebar. On small screens, select Saved to open it and × to close it.
